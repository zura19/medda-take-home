**Summary:**

A patient is evaluated for frequent, one-sided, throbbing headaches occurring 6–7 times a month, accompanied by nausea and light sensitivity. The patient previously used sumatriptan but stopped due to chest tightness, relying instead on ibuprofen and rest. 

The doctor diagnoses migraines and recommends the following management plan:
*   **Preventive medication:** Start propranolol 40 mg once daily.
*   **Acute treatment:** Continue ibuprofen (400 mg), but limit use to a maximum of 10 days per month to prevent rebound headaches.
*   **Monitoring:** Maintain a headache diary.
*   **Follow-up:** Schedule a visit in six weeks.
*   **Red flags:** Seek immediate emergency care if experiencing the "worst headache of your life" or sudden one-sided weakness.
