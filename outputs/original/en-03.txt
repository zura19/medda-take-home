The patient visited the doctor after sustaining an ankle injury while playing football. Upon examination, the doctor diagnosed a **grade 2 lateral ankle sprain** and determined that an X-ray was unnecessary based on the Ottawa ankle rules.

The doctor prescribed a treatment plan consisting of the **RICE method** (rest, ice, compression, and elevation), the use of crutches for a few days, and ibuprofen for pain management. The patient was referred to physiotherapy and advised that they could likely return to sports in three to four weeks, provided the injury does not worsen.
