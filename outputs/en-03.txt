CHIEF COMPLAINT
Ankle injury

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
Patient rolled ankle playing football yesterday evening. Patient reports rapid swelling and was able to walk a few steps with a limp immediately after the injury.

CURRENT MEDICATIONS
None

ALLERGIES
Not mentioned

VITALS AND EXAM
Swelling and bruising on the outside of the ankle. Tenderness over the ligament but not over the bone. Patient able to take 4 steps.

ASSESSMENT
Grade 2 lateral ankle sprain

PLAN
Rest, ice for 20 minutes every few hours, use a compression bandage, and keep ankle elevated. Take ibuprofen 400 mg 3 times daily with food for up to 5 days. Use crutches for the first few days. Referral to physiotherapy.

FOLLOW-UP
Return if unable to put weight on the ankle or if pain worsens.

PATIENT SUMMARY
You have a grade 2 lateral ankle sprain from your football injury. Rest, ice, elevate, and use a compression bandage and crutches as directed, and take 400 mg of ibuprofen 3 times daily with food for up to 5 days. You will be referred to physiotherapy and should be able to return to sports in 3 to 4 weeks. Please return to the clinic if you cannot put weight on your ankle or if your pain gets worse.
