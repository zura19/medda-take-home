CHIEF COMPLAINT
Headaches

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
Patient experiences 6 or 7 headaches a month that are one-sided and throbbing, accompanied by feeling sick and light sensitivity. Patient previously used sumatriptan and stopped it about 2 months ago because it caused chest tightness. Currently takes ibuprofen and lies down in the dark.

CURRENT MEDICATIONS
Ibuprofen

ALLERGIES
No known allergies

VITALS AND EXAM
Blood pressure is 118/74. Neurological exam is normal.

ASSESSMENT
Migraines

PLAN
Start propranolol 40 mg 1 time daily. Keep a headache diary. For attacks, ibuprofen 400 mg is fine, limited to no more than 10 days a month to avoid rebound headaches.

FOLLOW-UP
Follow up in 6 weeks. Go straight to the emergency department if you get the worst headache of your life or weakness on 1 side of your body.

PATIENT SUMMARY
You have one-sided throbbing headaches with nausea and light sensitivity about 6 or 7 times a month, which are assessed as migraines. You should start taking propranolol 40 mg once a day, keep a headache diary, and limit ibuprofen 400 mg to no more than 10 days a month. Follow up in 6 weeks.
