CHIEF COMPLAINT
Ankle injury

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
Patient rolled ankle while playing football yesterday evening. Reported rapid swelling and ability to walk a few steps with a limp immediately following the injury.

CURRENT MEDICATIONS
None

ALLERGIES
Not mentioned

VITALS AND EXAM
Swelling and bruising on the lateral aspect of the ankle. Tenderness over the ligament, no tenderness over the bone. Patient able to take 4 steps.

ASSESSMENT
Grade 2 lateral ankle sprain

PLAN
Rest, ice for 20 minutes every few hours, compression bandage, and elevation. Take ibuprofen 400 mg 3 times daily with food for up to 5 days. Use crutches for the first few days. Referral to physiotherapy.

FOLLOW-UP
Return if unable to bear weight or if pain worsens.

PATIENT SUMMARY
You have a grade 2 lateral ankle sprain from your football injury. You should rest, ice, elevate, and use a compression bandage and crutches for the first few days, along with taking ibuprofen 400 mg 3 times daily for up to 5 days. You may be able to return to sports in 3 to 4 weeks. Please return to the clinic if you cannot put weight on your ankle or if your pain gets worse.
