CHIEF COMPLAINT
Sore throat and painful swallowing.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
Patient reports throat pain for 3 days. Reports fever of 38.6 °C last night. Denies cough.

CURRENT MEDICATIONS
None

ALLERGIES
Penicillin (hives).

VITALS AND EXAM
Temperature 38.4 °C. Tonsils swollen with white patches. Tender lymph nodes in neck. Rapid strep test positive.

ASSESSMENT
Strep throat.

PLAN
Prescribed azithromycin: 500 mg on day 1, then 250 mg once daily for 4 days. Take paracetamol for pain. Stay home until 24 hours of antibiotic treatment and fever resolution.

FOLLOW-UP
Return immediately if unable to swallow liquids or if experiencing trouble breathing. Otherwise, return in 1 week if symptoms do not improve.

PATIENT SUMMARY
You have strep throat. Take the prescribed azithromycin and use paracetamol for pain. Stay home until you have been on the antibiotic for 24 hours and your fever is gone. Seek medical care immediately if you cannot swallow liquids or have trouble breathing, or return in 1 week if you are not feeling better.
